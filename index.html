Who am I?

A growing developer with a creative mindset

I am Zachary Scott, a high school student from Brisbane, Australia, with a strong interest in design, digital creativity, and software development.

I am currently relearning web development, problem-solving, and user-focused design. I enjoy building things that are useful, thoughtful, and visually engaging.

What motivates me most is the combination of creativity and logic: turning ideas into experiences, and learning how technology can solve problems in practical and meaningful ways. I also find interesting in creating experimental user experiences for websites such as Billy-Bit and zsdev..
